# July 2025 Report

An acquaintance of mine from online recently sent me a whole list of questions on Telegram, under the guise of: "A friend’s monthly check-in—please provide an update on how you’re doing by answering the following:"
- How was your month?
- Did you drink enough water?
- Is your moral state still breathing or has it been dead for some time?
- What was the most pleasant thing this month?
- And the opposite - what's the worst thing that's happened?
- On a scale of 0 to 5, what are your impressions of the month?

A couple of questions have been cut, since one is half-humorous and the other is based directly on that bit of humor. Well then, shall we get started?

### How was your month?

On the whole, it’s been pretty average—though things improved toward the end, even if I did end up spending more than usual. The ultra run at work is already halfway over, just need to finish August and the schedule will return to normal.

### Did you drink enough water?

I drank enough energy drinks, that's enough. Besides, the water source is in the kitchen, and an entire ecosystem has developed in there; I don’t want to die such a stupid death. I'll fix the situation with my salary.

### Is your moral state still breathing or has it been dead for some time?

My mood, much like my interests, tends to fluctuate, but, all in all, it aligns with my answer to the first question. Average, but better towards the end.

### What was the most pleasant thing this month?

I suppose the birthday I celebrated at my home was celebrated according to all the rules, with cake and candles.

### And the opposite - what's the worst thing that's happened?

Um, well, I guess there was a bit of a drinking session a couple of days before my birthday. I spent way too much money and got absolutely wasted—long story short, it was a blast.

### On a scale of 0 to 5, what are your impressions of the month?

Minus a point for the heat, minus a point for the expenses, plus a point for the get-togethers and a new acquaintance. I’d give it a four, I suppose.
