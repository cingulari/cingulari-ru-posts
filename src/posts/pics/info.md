# Need information on requests? Read below.

*last updated on June 28, 2026*

Well, first of all, thanks to those who ask me to do something, because this is good practice on a random topic.
One and a half, I will not take money on principle. This is just a hobby, and it will remain that way for the rest of my life.
Secondly, so you know and I can be at peace, here is a list of all the details of my presets:

- I **don’t draw porn and guro**. I am mediocre, and most importantly, I am not interested in these topics.

- I will never draw anything **illegal, criminal, fetishistic, and/or politicized**. The first and second are logical, the third is hindered by my... chastity (decency, puritanism, whatever you want to think of it, the gist is clear), and the fourth -- well, fuck, what country do I live in? Take a wild guess.

	- A sub-point to the previous one: **keep your politics to yourself, and don't bring them to me**. No funny windmills/bankais, no pretty colorful flags, no stands. My field is **standard orientation, lack thereof, and a few lesbians for fun**. However, I won't be scrutinizing the background of your requests, so I'm counting on you not to throw any banned bullshit at me.

- **A little nudity and injuries within the bounds of propriety are acceptable**. By nudity, I mean underwear, swimsuits, and anything along the lines of "naked but covered." Injuries include bruises, burns, blood, cuts, abrasions, and other non-lethal items.

- As you can see, I prefer **female characters**. It's not that I don't want to draw men, it's just that they end up looking even more bland.

- Body type -- **slender and normal**. Muscular ones still require a better understanding of anatomy, more chubby ones... yes, I can, of course, but I don't have any special training.

- My **weakest sides** are backgrounds, technology, animals, and industrial design. We'll get into that sometime.

- In terms of style, I prefer to stick to the standard **anime** one. Yes, yes, yes, I admit my inability to something more complex. However, I practice lineless.

- **Keep it simple**. No one wants to hear tons of unnecessary details, especially when there's no money involved.

- I like to sit and pore over AI slop stuff I generate locally. However, as I can see, **I definitely don't use AI for drawing**. I mostly do rough drafts and sometimes line work in a sketchbook, then scan and finish.

- You may not like me, and I can don't like you, that's okay. If it comes down to working together, I hope we can both endure it, make it beautiful, and then go our separate ways.

That's all for now, maybe I'll update later.
