### Trade 1
![](/img_orig/2025/10-trade1.png)
Drawn as part of an art trade with someone. In return, I got another Beato from Umineko. I really put some extra effort into this one—I tried adding a forest background, applying textures to the tights, and highlighting the gold stripe on the skirt. Not everything turned out exactly as I’d hoped, but for a piece completed in a single evening, I think it’s quite good; usually, something like this would have taken me about 15 hours in the past.
