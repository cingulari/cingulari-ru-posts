# Archived: mango/onime

Warning: contains graphic scenes of violence inflicted via pen on a notebook and mouse-driven GIMP manipulation. It’s certainly a bit better than the grim stuff found in the [stalker folder](../stalker), but you’ve been warned!

![](/img_orig/old/anime/1.jpg)

This is the oldest stuff I have. It dates back to the days when I was watching Naruto and barely had any presence on the internet, heck, I only got a VK account maybe a year ago at this point. I didn’t even have a standalone scanner or printer back then, so all these early pieces were photographed using the two-megapixel camera on a [Nokia 6300](https://wikipedia.org/wiki/Nokia_6300). Believe me I wouldn’t lie about something like this!

![](/img_orig/old/anime/6.jpg)

A slightly evolved version, now equipped with a hoodie, a backpack, and even legs. I won’t deny that this is pretty much what my own wardrobe looked like back in the 7th or 8th grade (hey, you backpackers, what exactly do you carry?). The character has now clearly taken on a female identity. Oh, and I tried to give the neck a more human-like design as much as I could, anyway, given my complete lack of desire to use an eraser.

![](/img_orig/old/anime/2.jpg)

Here is the "refined" version of the first image, the one where that "long-neck" affliction actually made its appearance. And the collar is just bizarre. Not to mention those chonki arms. And, well, there’s no waist, no breasts, no hips -- just a flat board devoid of any secondary sexual characteristics; the only reason we assume it’s a woman is because of the clothing. But that’s the only clue! **By the way, the next two images are of particularly poor quality though I suspect you’ve already spotted them, as they appear just a couple of lines below this text.**

![](/img_orig/old/anime/3.jpg)

Need I even mention that using a set of "Temu" 12-color pencils isn't exactly helping matters? Sure, I’ve gained a bit of experience, but the result is still pretty terrible. Meanwhile, our character has acquired a twin brother -- he’s inherited the same damn face and those amputated-looking legs. Combat boots, by the way, seem to have become my favorite footwear (and they really are). Also, I either remembered or just came up with a little shortcut for myself(on my own btw): instead of drawing hands, I can just tuck them into pockets, lol!
No folds, pillow-like shading, and the proportions are off. Could it get any worse?

![](/img_orig/old/anime/4.jpg)

Hahahaha, of course it can! Let’s set aside the fact that in the background we have some sort of placeholders instead of actual buildings, and a basketball hoop that’s shaped weirdly and isn’t even on the court, let’s just ignore that. And what do you think of these boobs? Or the head, which is bigger than the ball? We’ve got ourselves another illegitimate brother here -- trust ME, this one’s different; I created it myself, let’s call it the younger one. The proportions are more off kilter than ever, it honestly can’t get any worse, I guarantee it. I’ll be ditching the color soon, too, just bear with me a little longer. On the bright side, I do like the ball; as you might guess, I actually had a basketball when I was a kid. My favorite sport type.

![](/img_orig/old/anime/5.jpg)

Well, I finally got the nerve to draw myself into this whole scene. The pants were camouflage, of course, but the combat boots were still there. Also, could someone please tell him that lines meant to be straight should actually be straight and align with one another? I mean, look at the edge of the platform behind that 2D fence!

![](/img_orig/old/anime/7.jpg)

An advanced version of the woman from the very beginning. Sure, there are no folds or proper shading, but SERIOUSLY, WHY ARE YOU NITPICKING? There’s at least some basic shading, and that’s good enough. By the way, it’s kind of interesting to see what’s drawn on the other side of the sheet -- I can tell it’s a box, but I don’t remember exactly what it was supposed to be. Wait a minute... if I’m reading this right, it’s a reference to those crappy stalker-style drawings! A guy on some boxes, holding two pistols with his arms twisted all wrong! Let’s keep that in mind.

![](/img_orig/old/anime/8.jpg)

Older brother, black and white photo. Since I remembered he had headphones (he really did), I decided to add a music player to the drawing. Times were tough in our region back then; there were no iPods at our school, in fact, portable music players didn't really exist for us at all. So, using pictures from computer magazines as a reference, I drew something that looked like a CD player. Funnily enough, I actually own the exact same model now still in its original box! Hope my ex-gf didn't mind some stealing from her attic. She allowed it in first place.
As for the drawing itself, I’ve moved on from pencils for good, so any mistakes here are here to stay for all eternity.

![](/img_orig/old/anime/9.jpg)

The first signs of Naruto influence on these guys are starting to show. In my defense, I couldn't care less about Konoha(in fact, fuck konoha and all its villagers) so my gang hail from Amegakure. They fled the place because and, mind you, this is a direct quote from my notes "they didn't like how things were run there." OmegaLUL, sounds a bit like some of the people who’ve relocated abroad these days (though, I should clarify, only some of them). The reflection in the puddle was supposed to show a ceiling with a hole in it, but, as usual, something went wrong.

![](/img_orig/old/anime/10.jpg)

Oh, one of my first attempts at torturing GIMP! Back then, whenever I wanted to create something, I’d use a couple of drawing programs: [Paint.NET](https://www.getpaint.net/) and [GIMP](https://www.gimp.org/). Ironically, while I did manage to trace the lines, I completely botched the job -- the goofy, vapid smile is a prime example. Plus, I completely lost track of the back of the cloak, which I’d already failed to account for back when I was sketching it on paper. Come to think of it, I have no idea why I chose such garish, intense colors. And by that time, I definitely had internet access—why didn’t I look up a proper skin tone instead of picking something that looks like it came straight out of The Simpsons?

I didn’t have a drawing tablet back then, so I did the tracing with a mouse, sometimes using the "line" tool.

![](/img_orig/old/anime/12.jpg)

Don’t ask how the katana stays on his back; I came up with the floating weapon concept long before Genshin Impact and its ilk. I think I messed up the color of his hand—and ended up turning it into a sort of pseudo-glove, so I came up with a backstory: after transforming into a skeleton(...-moron), he couldn’t fully restore his original appearance. And yes, the messy grid lines are a hint that there used to be text around him, but I decided to crop that out for this version.

![](/img_orig/old/anime/16.jpg)

Additional characters for the Naruto art above: a girl and her brother(-s, but only the youngest is shown here). I came up with a great idea -- let’s assume that whenever they cast their techniques, a dark chakra manifests across their skin. It’s also a convenient way to cover up the parts that are most difficult to draw, which is why they appear as a solid, black mass.

![](/img_orig/old/anime/15.jpg)

...An extremely... unfortunate iteration of my alter ego. It definitely came after the cloak-wearing version. Well, feast your eyes on this bloated mug and those hideous glasses which I don’t even wear. I actually thought I’d scrubbed every trace of this embarrassing mess, but apparently not.

![](/img_orig/old/anime/14.jpg)

But this is exactly the thing, after the failure in reincarnation, since I already came up with it. I won’t deny that I’m not even good at basic anatomy, let alone skeletal structures. I actually had to peek at an anatomy reference just to make the hand look somewhat plausible. And we will conveniently cover the affected part of the face with bangs, which, of course, were not damaged.

The other vagabonds in the squad are all a proud 13 years old, so I’d wager I was 13, too. Probably.
