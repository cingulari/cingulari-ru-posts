# Archived: Left 2 Troll

Oh, and there's plenty to discuss and show here. The pictures will be separate, broken down by character where applicable. All the splash pages and group scenes are here.

>...I wanted to launch my own comic book - although at the very beginning I was thinking about something like “School 13”...
>The project was conceived around 2012. I was seriously studying Macromedia Flash at the time, and I wanted to create something in the spirit of "School 13." Initially, it was supposed to be a cartoon, but now it's going to be a short comic, and that MAYBE.


## Timeline

Or rather, what was left of it, heh-heh.
June 15, 2012 - Development began under the name "Down Town."
August 1, 2012 - The project was renamed from "Down Town" to "Left 2 Troll."
2012-2014 - More characters added.
2014-present - Clinical death. F.

## Plot

The action was supposed to take place in a post-apocalyptic zombie world. Here, as in "Last of Ass," they don't pose much of a threat; they retain their wits, but their intelligence is certainly lacking. Our anal jokesters, Sakry and Dest, decided to exploit this: after all, normal people might get angry with their toilet humor, but here we have only harmless zombies who easily fall for any, even the most stupid, nonsense. The humor was supposed to be built on the contrast between two idiots with /b/-level jokes and the unfortunate, afflicted zombies who would very much like to reintegrate into society and be left alone by this pair of degenerates.
The rest of the cast has a neutral-positive attitude toward zombies, but mostly ignores the jabs directed at them.

### Splashart

Don't beat me with slippers for my shitty humor, my neighbor and I were inspired by School 13, let me remind you.
![first splash](/img_orig/old/l2t/3.jpg)

Here we're introduced to the two main characters—at the time, their names were Rambler and Translate, and the project itself wasn't "Left2Troll" but "Down Town," which kind of hints at that. Old demotivators are present, as are mentions of rage faces. I was little (well, not that much anymore; in 2012, I was definitely 15), and I tried not to be too obscene on the pieces of paper that might be seen by *extra* eyes, so there will be some swear words, of course, but not much. And anyway, it was so nice when humor and other stuff could be constructed without "fuck" every five words. Although, maybe I'm just tired.
Nevertheless, what characters do we have in the picture? Rambler is a punk with a mohawk, Translate has a more standard appearance, but is always laughing. I think Beavis and Butthead also left their influence here.

![second splash](/img_orig/old/l2t/4.jpg)
The second splash was styled like some kind of recruitment ad for a project. You know, if I'd been stupid enough and brave enough to post this, maybe we could have done something volunteer-based, like a couple of spreads(for my ass). Stale memes are present, as are mentions of Kenny, stolen jokes, and other joys of life.
Among the characters, we have Lisa, who hasn't yet changed her name to Lonely. This isn't her first appearance, but more details are in the section dedicated specifically to her.

![third splash](/img_orig/old/l2t/5.jpg)
The focal point of this section is the diner "Ashot's" with its memes about kitten shawarma. But look at these brave men, both Ashotik and Gordon, motherfucker Freeman! And what a kitty, indeed. The cat is fine, he was just there for the comic effect of the joke about kitten meat, you probably didn't get it right away. The kitty wasn't harmed and won't be harmed, I swear by mother, ezhi. And so, even though he's a very minor character, Ashot personally receives my award for best character on the project.
Of course, this award should have gone to Gordon, but he's keeping quiet. Well, you get it, right?

![fourth splash](/img_orig/old/l2t/6.jpg)
And the last splash in this series is, unfortunately, the most bland. Just a booth with the cashier, Marya, counting down the days until her quitting. They should have definitely added some decor to the store, otherwise they've gotten completely lazy. The sign for the guns mentions left4dead2, which ultimately led to the change of scenery to post-apocalyptic and the name to left2troll.
