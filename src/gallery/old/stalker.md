# Archived: stalker

My mug right now, and I’m not joking:

## <a id="1"></a> Scans
### Campfire gathering
![](/img_orig/old/stalker/1.jpg)
I remember drawing this mess before class. My buddies and I were cracking up over the overturned truck and the one-armed stalker. He was actually supposed to be pointing at the camera, but I just couldn't pull off the drawing. And the scale is a total disaster, too -- the stalkers who are sitting down end up looking bigger than the one-armed guy.
### Rookie village
![](/img_orig/old/stalker/2.jpg)
One of the first masterpieces based on stalker. I think it still holds up pretty well today. Sure, the artwork is crap and the scale is all over the place but honestly, I couldn't care less about scale anyway. On the flip side, it features a decent-looking log with a wood texture, fairly normal-looking guns, and even some blur effect in the attic as much as was possible, at least. The funniest detail has to be the PDA strapped to the stalker’s sleeve.
### They’re hitting us from the rear, the bastards!
![](/img_orig/old/stalker/3.jpg)
My attempt at drawing poses other than people sitting around a campfire with a guitar. It’s a disaster, of course, but that hasn’t stopped me—and it won’t stop me in the future. In the middle, there’s something resembling stacks of concrete slabs (but why?). As for the proportions, well, let’s just say they’re... something else. And the space itself is completely nonsensical. The design of the guns will likely remain similar to these barrels lying around for quite some time to come.
### Don’t trust, don’t fear, don’t ask
![](/img_orig/old/stalker/4.png)
My vision of what Sidorovich’s den might look like after his death. Take a look at the stalker’s powerful arms—I tried to give them a design that feels truly human. **And honestly, go fuck yourselves; I came up with the Coprorate(not a typo) Memphis concept before it even became mainstream.** This little crap was uploaded as stalker-themed concept art for a browser game called Mutant, where people mostly just gawked at the Kalashnikov broken in two. The game’s group and the game itself has long since shut down, yet I was still getting notifications about new comments and favs as recently as 2022. Starting with this piece, I began drawing artifact containers without any regard for whether their placement actually made sense. Also, hanging from the stalker’s belt, there’s something that looks like the tentacles of a bloodsucker.
### Stuff
![](/img_orig/old/stalker/5.jpg)
The raw, authentic expanses of the Garbage serve as the setting for this scene. An unknown sniper helps a stalker fend off a bloodsucker by using a poop-bullet. The idea is that the backpack caught inside an electra anomaly contains such valuable loot that the risk is well worth it.
### Night Watch
![](/img_orig/old/stalker/6.jpg)
Here’s another authentic corner of the Garbage. We have a box-like structure here—the kind you’d build on your first night in Minecraft, which serves as the local bar, "1800 Rads." Both the name and the design are original creations. In the foreground and background, there are precarious-looking watchtowers used for keeping guard. Each sentry has an alarm button, though, to be honest, that button is nowhere near as alarming as the situation for the stalker standing on a tower that lacks any kind of perimeter railing.
### At home
![](/img_orig/old/stalker/7.jpg)
The ruins of a house, a barrel serving as a fire pit, and three stalkers relaxing as if it were an everyday occurrence next to the corpse of a bloodsucker, which had been crushed by one concrete block and decapitated by another. I’m not exactly the sharpest tool in the shed; I even managed to draw one string on the guitar, despite it being turned away from us. The stalkers didn’t end up positioned quite right around the fire, but what can you do?

### Monostals 1
![](/img_orig/old/stalker/21.jpg)
I came up with a clan for the Zone that, according to its lore, is completely neutral toward all other factions except for Duty, with whom they are in an explicit conflict. The clan is called "Monostals." I also decided to draw a couple of stalkers representing this clan in various (though essentially identical) poses. I even threw in a "summer beach" version for some reason.
### Monostals 2
![](/img_orig/old/stalker/22.jpg)
And here are a couple more stalkers for the Monostals clan. The second one’s excessively long arms aren’t a bug, they’re a feature. Same goes for his square face. Back then, I used to draw heads using literally just two lines; I think that’s pretty noticeable. I should also mention that it was around this point that I finally started to suspect that such ridiculously long arms were clearly a miscalculation,  one that needed to be fixed.
