### tahnok-va
![](/img_orig/2024/6.png)
The next victim was Tahnok-Va; working on it reminded me what "gradient maps" are all about.
I recall seeing Brandon from the BJG channel change color palettes on the fly for his pixel art; I think I even used that gradient technique myself for a couple of houses.
There are some issues, but they’d likely vanish if the canvas were larger. Also, it’s best not to mix highly contrasting colors, it’s better to stick to compatible ones when creating gradients.
But the fuck up came out as planned.

### Captain (Nox)
![](/img_orig/2024/captain.png)
Let's dilute the flow of women (and faces that look extremely similar to them) with the likeness of an old man. All joking aside, this page needs strong men -- after all, it’s weak people who have brought about these difficult times, and well, you know the rest.
Meet Jandor, an airship captain from Nox, released in 2000. A legendary warrior, he managed long before the game’s events to level the playing field between humans and necromancers (with their undead minions), after which he sailed off into the sunset to pursue a new career as a captain. Given the typical demographic of this site and the general obscurity of the game it’s safe to assume this character won’t be familiar to most of you.
I think I’ll leave this as just a rough sketch for now. Older characters are notoriously difficult for someone as talentless as me to draw; you have to account for a bit of a paunch, a shorter stature, and plenty of wrinkles. In this case, I ruthlessly shortened his legs by a quarter—in the original concept art, he appears as a rather stocky, stout little fellow.
As for Hecubah I might just consider bringing her to life as well, heh.
